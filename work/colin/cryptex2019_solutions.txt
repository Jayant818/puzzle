Editor Photo

The books pictured in the background are the relevant items for this puzzle. The note in the caption,
“counting on you”, is a hint to guide you toward treating them as if they were an abacus.

In a normal abacus, the beads below the tally line (the center) have a value of 1, and the beads above
the tally line have a value of 5. The beads moved to the center are those that are included in the tally.
The beads toward the outside edges are not.

Of course, for this puzzle, the layout is a modified version, having more 5s than would be typical.

Each shelf is treated independently. The left side of the shelf is the lower portion of the abacus (1) and
the right side is the upper portion (5). If it helps, you can visualize each bookcase rotated to the left
(counter-clockwise).

Tallying each of the shelves:

   2        5     15     23      21      12      6       6      15       5
   B        E     O      W       U        L      F       F      O        E

Room Escape Divas

This puzzle has an odd layout with areas marked on the top and bottom and along the sides. If you
connect the top and bottom markings, it highlights the words “mark” and “spot” – a reference to
“x marks the spot”.

Connect the lines on the left and right sides of the page to make what appear to be a musical grand
staff.

You will also note that the three rightmost Xs happen to fall exactly on the lines, indicating that the last
line of text would occur on the ledger line that’s between the upper and lower staff.

Since you have an upper staff and a lower staff, the upper staff is most likely treble. Finding the Xs
gives the notes C C E G G.

Humming or playing or searching the web for this sequence of notes will lead you to the Blue Danube
Waltz.




Solution:
BLUE DANUBE
